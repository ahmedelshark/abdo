About Our Company

SAS VET was established in 2017 in Beheira Governorate, Arab Republic of Egypt. The company operates in trading, importing, and distributing veterinary and agricultural products, as well as toll manufacturing through licensed third-party manufacturers under official agreements.

The company follows a smart expansion strategy beginning with local market coverage in the Nile Delta region, gradually expanding nationwide, and implementing a phased export plan targeting Arab, Islamic, and African countries, followed by European and American markets.

SAS VET is committed to supporting local and regional economic growth by providing safe and effective products -with competitive prices- that improve animal health and agricultural productivity, aligned with sustainable development principles.

As part of its international expansion strategy, SAS VET Egypt maintains non-binding cooperative relationships with: SASS VET – Kingdom of Saudi Arabia SAS VET USA – United States of America Both entities operate as independent legal enterprises, while the owner and CEO of SAS VET Egypt maintains administrative and advisory ties to facilitate knowledge exchange and strategic support.

Our Vision

To be a leading company in local and international markets in the field of veterinary and agricultural products through smart and measured expansion, full commitment to quality standards, and providing innovative solutions that meet market needs and support food security، economic growth, and sustainable development.

Our Mission

SAS VET aims to provide safe and effective veterinary and agricultural products that reflect the latest global advancements, supporting the development of the veterinary and agricultural sectors and enhancing efficiency and quality in production. Our products are manufactured to the highest international standards, in compliance with the regulatory requirements of target markets, and are officially registered with the relevant authorities. The company follows a structured expansion strategy across local and global markets to promote animal health and contribute to economic growth at the local, Arab, Islamic, African, and international levels.

Our Core Values

  • Quality and Commitment: We are committed to applying the highest standards of quality across all stages of work, offering only registered and approved products.

  • Integrity and Transparency: We build our relationships with clients and partners on honesty, clarity, and mutual trust.

  • Sustainable Partnerships: We believe in long-term collaboration based on shared interests, contributing to the development of the economies in which we operate.

  • Smart Growth and Professionalism: We pursue measured, gradual expansion that ensures stability and quality, following systematic and organized procedures in all aspects of our operations.

  • Legal and Regulatory Compliance: We fully comply with all legal and regulatory requirements, both locally and internationally.

  • Positive Impact: We strive to create added value for society, the economy, and the environment through everything we do.
